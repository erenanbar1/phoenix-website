Our Game

Tilt Ball

Tilt the platform to guide the ball to the goal.

Tilt Ball is a 2D physics-flavoured puzzle game. A ball sits on a stick hung from two ropes. Raise the left or right end to tilt it, and the ball rolls along it. Climb the stick up the screen and drop the ball into the winning hole — while dodging lose holes and other obstacles along the way.

  • 🎚️
    Two ropes, two thumbsHold the left or right button to lift that end of the stick.
  • ⚖️
    Real momentumThe ball rolls with the tilt. Overdo it and it flies off the end.
  • 🕳️
    Win holes & lose holesOnly one hole is the goal. The rest will swallow your run.
  • 🧲
    Obstacles that fight backMagnets, portals, moving hazards and more as you climb higher.

About the Studio

Small team. No shortcuts.

Phoenix Entertainment Studios was founded on a simple bet: a small, obsessive team can out-craft a big one. No committees, no filler content, no games we wouldn't play ourselves.

Tilt Ball went through the same fire every idea of ours does — rapid prototyping, brutal internal playtesting, and a refusal to ship anything that doesn't feel great in the first ten seconds.
