About the Studio

Small team. No shortcuts.

Phoenix Entertainment Studios was founded on a simple bet: a small, obsessive team can out-craft a big one. No committees, no filler content, no games we wouldn't play ourselves.

Every game we ship goes through the same fire — rapid prototyping, brutal internal playtesting, and a refusal to launch anything that doesn't feel great in the first ten seconds.
